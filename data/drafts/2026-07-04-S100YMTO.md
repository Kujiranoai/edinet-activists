# Oasis Discloses 5.07% Stake in Infomart, Flags Governance Dialogue and Possible Further Accumulation

Oasis Management Company Ltd. filed a **大量保有報告書 / Large Shareholding Report** on **July 2, 2026** for **Infomart Corporation / 株式会社インフォマート (2492.T)**. The filing says the reporting obligation arose on **June 25, 2026**. Oasis reports holding **13,568,100 shares**, equal to **5.07%** of share certificates, etc. Because this is an initial large shareholding report, the prior holding ratio is reported as “—”; therefore, the change from the prior known filing is **not available / not applicable**.

## Why it may matter

**Commentary:** This filing is notable because it marks Oasis crossing Japan’s 5% large-shareholding disclosure threshold in Infomart. Oasis also uses explicit “important proposal” language, stating that its purpose includes constructive dialogue with management to improve medium- to long-term corporate value and corporate governance.

The filing says Oasis has already proposed matters related to **important asset disposal** and **abolition of all or part of a business**, and that it plans to propose certain additional matters over the next 12 months. These include categories relating to management appointments or dismissals, board composition changes, major reorganizations, business transfers or discontinuations, delisting, and certain acquisition-related matters.

**Commentary:** The language suggests a potentially engaged shareholder posture, but the filing should not be read as proof of a specific activist campaign beyond what is disclosed. The document does not provide detailed proposals, transaction terms, or a timetable for any governance outcome.

The filing also states that, as part of portfolio investment, Oasis plans an act that would increase its holding ratio in Infomart common shares by more than 5 percentage points through on- and off-market transactions. This is subject to conditions, including whether the market price is judged undervalued. Specific price, quantity, and timing remain under consideration.
